# KnowledgeHUB™ Demo Checklist — Core Adviser Features

1. **Login & Home** — log in, show News Bulletins scroller, quick links, CPD summary widget, birthday card (if applicable).

2. **Learning tab**
   - View Live: join a Zoom link, note it auto-logs 50/50 CPD.
   - Weekly / Induction / Revalidation recordings.
   - Monthly Newsletters — open one, show it logs actual read time as CPD.
   - Industry Reading — timed CPD while article tab is open.
   - Knowledge Tests — take a short test, show pass/fail and CPD log entry.
   - Fitness & Properness questionnaire.

3. **AutoCPD™** (Compliance tab) — show the running CPD log, actual watch/read time entries, and the prompt to annotate an automated entry.

4. **Compliance reporting** — walk through one of the six REPORTING forms (e.g. Complaint or Gifts & Hospitality), submit, then show it landing in "My Submissions" with the red-dot unread indicator.

5. **Consumer Duty** — open the Consumer Duty card on Broker Profile, show a response card with flags and the AI-generated summary.

6. **Whereabouts** — set today's status (Office/Home/Out/Off/Out to External meeting/Holiday) from the homepage card.

7. **My Holiday** — show taken/allocated/carried-over/balance, submit a request, show the notification flow.

8. **PDF tools** — generate one on the spot: business card, moving card, or CPD record.

9. **Help widget (Alex)** — ask it something like "how many brokers do we have" or "who has the most reviews" to show the AI assistant live.

10. **My Account** — show profile fields, licence flags (Mortgages/Protection/CAS), and password/2FA setup.

*Optional closer if there's time:* Introducers or Other Business Interests form, or the Social Trust Content / Post Builder tools.
