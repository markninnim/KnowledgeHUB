v3.0 Admin One-to-One Tool, Non-Supervising Directors & My Team Fixes 8 August 2026

Admin & Paraplanner cards in My Team now open a dedicated one-to-one page instead of the broker profile, giving supervisors a place to log notes and action points and to log/approve holiday requests. Also added a "Non-supervising Directors" section to My Team, reassigned Torie Maleganeas as sole supervisor for Matt Stephens's former team, and fixed several My Team display bugs.

  • New Admin & Paraplanner one-to-one page: clicking one of these cards now opens notes/action-points and a holiday request/approve workflow instead of the broker profile, since admin staff don't have CPD, leads, or Feefo data. Notes and holiday requests are stored in two new Airtable tables (Admin One-to-One Notes, Holiday Requests) so they persist across redeploys.
  • Holiday requests are logged by a supervisor against a person and then approved or declined from the same page, with an optional annual allowance and a running total of days approved this year.
  • Added a "Non-supervising Directors" section to My Team (Supervise), listing Mark Ninnim, Terry McCutcheon, Aisling Oates, and Matt Stephens — senior staff who don't have a team to supervise but shouldn't be mixed in with rank-and-file adviser cards.
  • Reassigned Torie Maleganeas as the sole supervisor for Matt Stephens's former team of 15 advisers; Matt moved into Non-supervising Directors but keeps Supervise access.
  • Fixed Torie Maleganeas being misrouted into the "Compliance Support" sidebar group instead of "Supervisors" — her job title matched a compliance-detection rule meant for a different group of staff.
  • Fixed inconsistent card styling in My Team: Non-supervising Directors cards now match Supervisor cards' background colour, all cards are a consistent height regardless of content, and adviser names are a consistent navy colour across every card and list-view row (previously grey on supervisor cards).
  • User Management search now only starts filtering once 3 or more characters are typed, instead of filtering on every keystroke.
v2.9 User Engagement Panel Replaces Feature Rollout Controls 8 August 2026

Replaced User Management's Feature Rollout Controls panel with a new User Engagement panel: how many people are online right now, the highest number ever online at once, how many users have actually set a password, and how many have 2FA enabled — plus a "Creation cost since launch" figure added to Alex's Tools tab and every AI cost figure across the site now shown in £ instead of a mix of $ and £.

  • Added a lightweight in-memory "who's active" tracker (5-minute window) updated on every authenticated request, backing a live "Online now" count on User Management — resets on redeploy, since it's only meant for a live snapshot, not historical reporting.
  • Added a new Airtable table, Site Stats, to durably record the all-time peak number of concurrent users (survives Railway redeploys, unlike the in-memory tracker) — only writes when an actual new peak is hit, so it adds no load to normal requests.
  • "Logged in & set up" and "2FA enabled" counts reuse the same checks already used per-user in the User Management list (has a password set, has a TOTP secret), just totalled up for a quick company-wide view.
  • Removed the Feature Rollout Controls panel and its toggle grid from User Management — the underlying feature-flag system (used to hide/show nav tabs) is untouched and still stored in Airtable, just no longer editable from this UI.
  • Added a "Creation cost since launch (19 June 2026)" figure to Alex's Tools tab: fixed subscription costs multiplied by how many 19th-of-the-month billing dates have passed, plus Alex's all-time AI spend, both in £.
  • Standardised every AI cost figure across Alex's Salary tab, Alex's Tools tab, and the legacy API Usage admin modal to show £ (converted from the underlying USD pricing) instead of a mix of $ and £, which was confusing to compare at a glance.
v2.7 CPD AI Summary Rewrite, Download Modal & Sim Branding on AI Summaries 2 August 2026

Rewrote the whole-team AI CPD summary to focus on individual adviser completion rather than company-wide hours, replaced the Supervise toolbar's date pickers and Export CSV with a single Download button offering CSV/Excel/PDF and a name-or-all filter, added the bobbing Sim mascot with a consistent title to every AI-generated summary on the site, and gave Fitness & Properness submissions a red-dot notification so an auto-archived "Clear" submission can't quietly go unseen.

  • AI CPD Summary now reports how many advisers (and what percentage) have personally reached the correct year-to-date CPD pace, split out for Mortgage and Protection, instead of citing total hours logged company-wide — company-wide totals don't reflect whether the scheme is actually working for each individual.
  • Replaced the always-visible From/To date pickers and "Export CSV" button in Supervise with a single "Download" button that opens a modal: choose CSV, Excel (XLS), or PDF, choose a specific adviser (via a type-ahead name filter) or the whole team, and pick a date range — PDF and XLS exports are generated server-side with no new dependencies.
  • Added the bobbing Sim mascot icon and a consistent title to every AI-generated summary panel on the site: the whole-team CPD summary, the Compliance Log overview, both Consumer Duty summaries (adviser-facing and firm-wide), and the per-adviser Consumer Duty summary on Broker Profile.
  • Instructed every AI summary prompt to write plain hyphens instead of em/en dashes, for consistent, plainer formatting across the site.
  • Added a "Supervisor Unread" flag to Fitness & Properness submissions: new submissions (flagged or auto-archived as Clear) now show a red dot on the sidebar link and next to the submission itself until a supervisor actually opens it, so a clean submission being auto-filed away doesn't mean it goes unseen.
v2.6 Fitness & Properness Reliability Fix, Readable Airtable Data & Own Supervisor Page 1 August 2026

Fixed the Fitness & Properness form getting stuck on "Submitting…" or failing outright, removed an email notification the form no longer needs, made every submission readable straight from the Airtable table instead of a raw JSON blob, and gave Fitness & Properness its own dedicated page under Supervise > Compliance instead of sharing one with Questionnaire Feedback.

  • Fixed the Fitness & Properness Declaration getting stuck on "Submitting…" or failing with "Submission failed" — the compliance notification email had no timeout, so a slow or unreachable mail server blocked the whole request; added connection timeouts to the mail transporter and made the email fire-and-forget everywhere it's used.
  • Removed the Fitness & Properness compliance notification email entirely — submissions are reviewed in-app via Supervise > Compliance, so the extra email was unnecessary.
  • While auditing every place the site sends email, found and fixed a genuinely broken one: the "Share" button on Advice Standards sections called an email function that didn't exist anywhere in the code, so it silently failed every time a supervisor tried to use it.
  • Added 14 new columns to the Fitness & Properness Submissions Airtable table (one Yes/No + one detail column per disclosure question, plus a Flagged checkbox) so compliance can read a submission directly in Airtable without decoding a JSON string.
  • Gave Fitness & Properness its own page under Supervise > Compliance, matching the Introducers and Other Business Interests pages with broker/date filters, summary stat chips, and an accordion list — previously it was tucked inside the bottom of Questionnaire Feedback.
v2.5 Mortgage Import Automation, Data Integrity Audit & Annual Reporting Overhaul 1 August 2026

Added an in-app monthly Mortgage Completions import, ran a full audit of every Airtable field reference in the app (catching and fixing a broken field that was silently failing every Add/Edit User save), and rebuilt Fitness & Properness into a proper Annual Reporting suite with two new declarations and on-screen signatures.

  • Added an in-app monthly Mortgage Completions Excel import under AutoCRM™, available to supervisors and admins, so the monthly upload no longer depends on a local file or a developer running it manually.
  • Audited every Airtable table and field ID referenced in server.js against the live schema across all bases — found and fixed a broken field reference (F_SURVEYING) that had been silently causing every Add User and Edit User save to fail with a 500 error, and removed a second dead reference.
  • Moved Fitness & Properness submissions and Whereabouts manual edits off local server files and into Airtable, so both now survive Railway redeploys instead of resetting on every push.
  • Split the Fitness & Properness questionnaire into three separate Annual Reporting declarations under Compliance > Reporting: Fitness & Properness, Introducers, and Other Business Interests — each with its own on-screen signature capture, recorded permanently in Airtable.
  • Added Supervisor review pages for all three Annual Reporting declarations under Supervise > Compliance, with broker and date-range filters and an animated accordion layout showing full submission detail, including the captured signature.
  • Fixed the Adviser Unread indicator on My Submissions not clearing after being read — the "mark as seen" check was silently failing due to a field-lookup mismatch, so the red dot would reappear after reload even once viewed.
v2.4 Compliance Reporting Expansion & Reliability Fixes 31 July 2026

Rounded out the Compliance Reporting suite with two new report types and a firm-wide Compliance Log, gave advisers visibility into their own submissions, and fixed a run of UI reliability issues across Consumer Duty and Home.

  • Added Vulnerable Persons and SARs to the Compliance Reporting forms, and gave Compliance Log an editable status dropdown with Resolved entries archived out of the default view.
  • Added a firm-wide Compliance Log view under Supervise, with type and status filters and type-specific detail, plus compliance notes visible to the submitting adviser.
  • Added a "My Submissions" register so advisers can track the status and any compliance notes on their own reports, with a red-dot unread notification when compliance leaves an update.
  • Split Questionnaire Feedback into its own sidebar entry, scoped to the logged-in adviser's own responses, with a separate firm-wide copy retained under Compliance Log.
  • Replaced native dropdowns on the Compliance Reporting form with scrollable custom ones (native popups were clipping past the viewport on longer lists), and fixed a race condition that could silently lose a Consumer Duty checkbox tick.
  • Renamed Case Flags to Acre Case Flags, and switched several field lookups (compliance summary, user Products & Specialisms) to Airtable field IDs instead of names so future renames in Airtable can't silently break them.
  • Removed Surveying from the regulated Licences panel (it isn't a regulated licence) and fixed a Home dashboard column-alignment bug affecting the News Bulletins and CPD Summary cards.
v2.3 Sim Assistant Rebrand & Accurate CPD Logging 29–30 July 2026

Rebranded the help widget as Sim with new artwork and a livelier onboarding tour, and moved CPD logging away from flat time estimates towards actual time spent.

  • Rebranded KnowledgeHELPER as Sim, your personal assistant, with new vector artwork across the helper widget, chat panel, welcome modal and CPD modal, plus subtle bob animations throughout.
  • Restored onboarding as a full section-by-section modal welcome tour, with refreshed copy for every tab (Marketing, Learn, Earn, Pay, Engage, AutoCRM™, Surveying, ReEngage™).
  • CPD for videos and newsletters now logs actual watch/read time instead of a flat estimate, rounding up on short sessions so brief views still register.
  • Industry Reading CPD now logs automatically when the article tab is closed, and advisers are prompted to annotate automatically-logged CPD entries with what they learned.
  • Added an Employed adviser flag to the user Access section.
v2.2 Meeting Notes Automation, AI Meeting Recording & Mortgage Advice Standards V4 28 July 2026

Extended quarterly Meeting Notes into a full supervisor workflow — editable notes, an auto-email-ready Airtable setup, and AI-assisted meeting recording — and refreshed Mortgage Advice Standards to V4.

  • Meeting Notes can now be edited and deleted, not just added.
  • Renamed the AI-generated dashboard paragraph from "AI Supervisor Summary" to simply "Summary", and removed the self-review/segregation-of-duties framing from it — adviser self-review of their own compliance flags is approved practice at FPG and is no longer flagged as a concern; the summary now instead surfaces flag-type concentration worth discussing.
  • The Summary is now saved alongside Notes and Action Points on every Meeting Note record, plus new auto-populated Airtable fields (Action Points (Formatted), Supervisor Name, Note Taker Name, Recipient First Name) — enabling a one-click Airtable automation to email the adviser's supervisor after each 1:1.
  • Added in-browser meeting recording to the Add/Edit Note modal: supervisors can record a 1:1 directly, with the audio transcribed (OpenAI Whisper) and summarised into draft notes and action points (Claude) for review before saving.
  • Updated the Mortgage Advice Standards page fully from FPG's Mortgage Advice Standards V4 (July 2026), including the new Consumer Duty references, the full permitted-activities and consumer-credit tables, and the Annex B evidence-of-ID tables.
  • Refreshed the Mortgage Advice Standards knowledge test with updated and new questions reflecting the V4 content.
v2.1 Adviser Power BI Dashboards & Compliance Flag Log 24 July 2026

Gave supervisors a dedicated Power BI drill-down for each adviser from their broker profile, plus AI-generated compliance observations, quarterly meeting notes, and a flagged-case log.

  • Added a Power BI button to the Broker Profile page, opening a new per-adviser Adviser Dashboard populated from that adviser's own Power BI export data in Airtable.
  • Fixed adviser dashboard data lookup to read Airtable fields by name rather than a hardcoded field ID, so any adviser's table works correctly (not just the first one built).
  • Added an AI Supervisor Summary paragraph, generated live from each adviser's own case status, LTV, lender-share and flag data — highlighting pipeline bottlenecks, LTV risk skew, lender concentration and flag/self-review concerns.
  • Added quarterly Meeting Notes with agreed action points; incomplete action points automatically carry forward into the next quarter's note.
  • Added a Flag Log section sourced from the Power BI Flag Log page, showing each flagged case's type, status, flag type and created date.
  • Latest quarter's meeting notes and action points now also appear on the Broker Profile PDF export.
  • Refined dashboard card layout: top KPI cards at 33% width, all other card sections at 50/50.
