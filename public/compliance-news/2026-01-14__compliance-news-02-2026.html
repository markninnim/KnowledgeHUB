Compliance News 02/2026

Preferred CIC Provider – Guardian
14 January 2026

Background

We have a preferred CIC provider to save you the trouble of having to analyse the most suitable CIC product for each individual client.

We do periodic analyses (every 6 to 12 months) of CIC product features, benefits, illness definitions and claims data from all the insurers to determine whether we should maintain, or change, our preferred provider.

The Preferred CIC Provider – Guardian

Having conducted the above checks, we can confirm that Guardian are still our preferred CIC provider. Whilst we accept that in most instances the Guardian cover carries a higher premium, we also feel that the enhanced cover offered by their product shouldn't be a reason to discount it as our preferred provider.

Vitality SIC 2 and SIC 3

For those of you who prefer to recommend Vitality's Serious Illness Cover over Guardian's Critical Illness Cover, you can continue to do this where you are recommending SIC 2 or SIC 3.

Cheaper Alternatives

Where the cost of the Guardian CIC cover is prohibitive then you can of course consider cheaper alternatives for the client to choose. Our guidance here is that you don't always go straight from Guardian to the cheapest cover, as there are often better-quality products (than the cheapest) available at a price the client might consider acceptable.

Other relevant information – CIC Cancellation

As a reminder, where you are reviewing any existing Critical Illness Cover, with a view to possibly replacing that cover with new cover, please ensure that you are obtaining a CI Expert Comparison Report from either Tori, Richard, Dean or Pete.

For further information
