Record Keeping on lending into retirement cases
5 May 2026

Background

An analysis of our recent record keeping file checks has revealed there has been some common shortcomings in certain situations. Specifically, around residential cases where the mortgage is being extended beyond age 70, or perhaps even to age 75 or 80.

Our concerns relate to residential mortgages, rather than any self-funding BTL mortgages where the end date doesn't particularly matter.

Compliance views around Residential Lending Into Retirement cases

Where the client wishes for their mortgage to be the absolute lowest cost achievable, and where a lender allows it, we are seeing cases written to ages 70 or perhaps 75, or beyond that in some instances.

We are not saying that this approach is wrong. However, when we then see the client's retirement age in the fact find being extended to match the mortgage end date – purely to make the case fit within retirement – that is where we begin to question whether the fact find and suitability report genuinely reflect your discussions with the client.

In reality, most clients with a mortgage running to age 75 do not expect to still be working at 75. We would therefore expect a client's retirement plans – and their repayment plans where the mortgage extends later in life – to be fully explored, discussed, and challenged where appropriate.

We are asking that your records in these instances are more reflective of your genuine discussions – whether that's continuing to work, clearing the mortgage with pension lump sums or inheritance, shortening the term at future reviews, or downsizing.

Suggested Wording for Mortgages which extend to ages 70, 75, 80, etc

Suggested rationale paragraphs are available covering: one applicant continuing to work, clearing the final years using pension lump sums, an intention to shorten the term at future reviews, continuing to trade in a reduced capacity, an expected inheritance, and planned downsizing. Please speak with your Sales Manager for the full suggested wording.

Other Considerations

Whenever a mortgage is reviewed when a fixed rate is ending, please don't assume that the client will want to simply maintain their current mortgage term. Please use the opportunity to see if you can reduce their overall term.

Further information

Please do take the time to ensure your records on Acre correctly reflect your client discussions. If you are uncertain about any of this communication, or require training on our record keeping standards, please speak with your Sales Manager or with Tori Worthington, Richard Hamley or Pete Burgess.
