New ASU provider – Allianz
10 June 2026

We've made some Changes to our ASU Providers

From Monday 15th June 2026 Allianz will be replacing Wessex for new ASU quotes and applications.

So what is changing?

The new Allianz branded policy can be written on any of the following bases: Accident & Sickness, Accident, Sickness & Unemployment, or Unemployment only. Yes, we'll be able to recommend Unemployment only cover.

What about those clients with Wessex cover?

Their annually renewable policies will remain in place. Interestingly, both the Wessex branded ASU products and the Allianz branded ASU products are both underwritten by AmTrust in the background.

So, why sell ASU?

  1. It could be for clients who are unable to secure an underwritten Income Protection policy.
  2. It could be that the cost of IP with a short deferred period is expensive, so AS or ASU for the first 12 months followed by an IP policy with a 12 month deferred period might be a less expensive solution.
  3. Those clients who have been interested in Unemployment only cover, but for whom you've not been able to provide a U only product.

The ASU Rules from our Protection Advice Standards

Where you identify a need to protect an individual's income against being unable to work due to accident or sickness, an Income Protection product should be recommended in the first instance, rather than an AS policy. Where a client wishes to protect against unemployment, this requires an ASU product (or U only product).

Because of Prohibition Period rules, AS/ASU/U only recommendations linked to a 'Mortgage Sale' cannot be accepted/applied for until the LATER of the 8th day after the mortgage offer is received, or the 8th day after you have given a personalised illustration (10th day if posted) – unless the customer proactively requests to proceed at least 24 hours after receiving their illustration, without prompting from you.

You must not recommend joint ASU policies, as they can inhibit customers achieving their desired level of cover.

Unemployment Cover for the self-employed

Unemployment cover for the self-employed is not as straightforward as an employee being made redundant. Until further notice, only a small number of specialist Protection Only Advisers will be permitted to advise such clients – please refer via your Sales Manager.
