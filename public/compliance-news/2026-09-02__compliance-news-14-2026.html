Protection application best practice
2 September 2026

Protection Advisers only.

Background

We've recently received feedback and data from some of our protection providers highlighting a couple of areas where, as a business, we can improve our outcomes around cancelled from outset policies and non-disclosure related declines after application. To help ensure we're submitting the highest quality applications possible and protecting our clients from issues further down the line, please follow these best practices wherever possible.

Best practice processes

1. Let clients read medical questions themselves
Where appropriate, share your screen during the application process and allow the client to read the medical questions directly rather than reading them out on their behalf. This reduces the risk of questions being misread, misunderstood or inadvertently paraphrased, and helps ensure the client fully understands exactly what they are being asked before providing an answer.

2. Read back and reconfirm all answers
Once the health and lifestyle section has been completed, take a moment to read the answers back to the client and ask them to confirm that nothing has been missed, no matter how minor it may seem. It is always better to disclose something that later proves to be irrelevant than to omit information that could lead to complications at claim stage or result in the insurer revisiting the application.

A simple confirmation such as:

"Can you confirm that you've disclosed all medical conditions, symptoms, consultations, tests, referrals, medications and lifestyle information that could be relevant, no matter how minor you believe it to be?"

can be very effective.

3. Email the application to the client
Some colleagues have now adopted this process, with a copy of the email saved on to the Acre record. This puts the onus very much back on the client, and not you, in the event of a claim being declined for non-disclosure.

4. Encourage client engagement with provider portals
Many insurers now provide customers with a portal, login area or follow-up journey after the application has been submitted. Please actively encourage clients to engage with these systems and carefully review any information presented to them. This provides another opportunity for the client to verify their answers and highlight anything that may have been missed during the initial application. The more opportunities we create for clients to review and confirm their disclosures, the greater the likelihood of achieving accurate applications first time.

Why this matters

These simple steps can significantly reduce:

  • Non-disclosure related declines
  • Policies being cancelled from outset
  • Delays in underwriting
  • Difficult conversations with a client post-application

Most importantly, they help ensure our clients receive the cover they need and expect when making a claim.
