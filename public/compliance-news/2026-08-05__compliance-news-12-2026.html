Compliance News 12/2026

Mortgage Advice Standards
5 August 2026

Background

We periodically review our Advice Standards, and our most recent version (v4, July 2026) has been circulated for reference.

The changes

In addition to the use of MRWL being changed to Mortgage SL throughout the document, minor changes have also been made across the document, including: allowing specialist advisers to advise on commercial and bridging in-house, deposit monies added to evidence requirements, updated “selling to yourself” rules, donor ID added to gifting requirements, updated affordability-into-retirement wording reflecting recent Compliance News, Debt Consolidation updated to reflect “not with Interest Only”, Student Loan debt consolidation now requiring compliance sign-off rather than a blanket refusal, a new section on reviewing rates between application and completion, an example fee cap of £999 for regulated products, and updated record keeping requirements for down-valued cases and replacement products.

Action and Further information

Please familiarise yourself with this document, and if you have any queries concerning our Mortgage Advice Standards then please speak with your Sales Manager. Our Protection Advice Standards will also be updated in the coming month or so.
