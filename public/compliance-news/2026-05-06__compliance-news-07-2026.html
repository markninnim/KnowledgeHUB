Halifax Applications for A&B Properties
6 May 2026

Background

Halifax have products which are paying a £250 cashback if the client's property is A or B EPC rated.

But some clients are missing out on this offer

The products are not clearly labelled in Acre sourcing as “green”, meaning we are seeing cases where a non-green product is being recommended over a green product. The consequence of this is that the client is missing out on £250 cashback.

We accept that Halifax green products are not highlighted on the initial sourcing screen, but there will be a green product with the same rate when you source – it's just a case of clicking into the products to see which one is the green product.

Changes to the Halifax Application to help you choose the correct product

Halifax now show a crosscheck message where a standard product has been selected but the EPC rating indicates the property is eligible for a Green Home product: “Due to the property's confirmed EPC rating, the customer may now be eligible for other products. Please review with customer”, followed by a question asking whether you wish to proceed with the current product selection rather than a potentially preferable one.

Actions

Please simply be aware of the £250 Halifax cashback on A&B EPC rated properties. And please don't ignore the Halifax failsafe question in those instances where you mistakenly recommend a standard (non-green) product when sourcing, as it is unfair on an eligible client to be missing out on this cashback.
