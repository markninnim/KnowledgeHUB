AI in Mortgage Fraud
26 February 2026

Background

I'm pretty sure most of us have now dabbled to some extent with AI. This could be for fun, or it could be to help us in our day-to-day jobs.

Well, it most definitely stands to reason then that fraudsters are using AI to help them secure their mortgage borrowing. This is particularly worrying, because clearly this is going to make detecting fraud even harder in the future.

It is therefore really important to be making absolutely sure that the client is who they say they are. You can check the likes of social media channels and Companies House to help with this validation. Please also ensure that you always enter the applicant's correct mobile number, email address and postal address on to a lender's application, as their fraud checks will start with this basic personal information.

Nationwide AI recordings

Nationwide Building Society have kindly shared a couple of recordings giving their insights into this matter. We should all have had an email from the lender on this matter.

Action

Please can you ensure that you watch these videos in the coming couple of weeks. This is a total of just 30 minutes (CPD!) spread across both recordings.
