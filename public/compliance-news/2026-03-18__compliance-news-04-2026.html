Mandatory Documents in Acre
18 March 2026

Background

To assist with us maintaining compliant records with all the required documents being attached, we are about to go live with the next phase of “mandatory documents” on an Acre record.

There are no actual changes to the documents that you need to attach to a case, but it is the requirement to correctly label the documents, rather than perhaps just attaching the likes of bank statements as “other”.

You'll be aware that currently you need to attach a “mortgage offer letter” to move your case on to “offer received”.

What is changing?

For certain cases loaded from Monday 23rd March you will now be required to correctly label the following two mandatory document types:

  • Evidence of Income, i.e. payslips, P60s, SA302s, Accounts, etc, and
  • Bank Statements.

These are documents that you have always been uploading to your cases, but now they will need to be added to your Acre record before you can progress the case to “application submitted”.

These documents will not be mandatory for Product Transfer cases where we waive them for existing clients in certain situations. Also, for BTLs, our documentation requirements are unchanged, but BTLs are not included in this first phase of ensuring documents are being labelled correctly.

Is it just these two additional documents?

No. Once this process has been properly embedded, we will then add some further documents which will be made mandatory to progress a case. But again, they will not be documents which are not already mandatory for a case anyway.

Some compliance considerations

Just a reminder that we still expect your cases to be loaded on Acre within 10 working days of the business being applied for. It is really important that you achieve this to avoid a breach being recorded for the Suitability Report not being sent to the client in a timely manner.

Please also ensure that your pipeline cases are progressed through the stages of Acre in a timely manner too.
