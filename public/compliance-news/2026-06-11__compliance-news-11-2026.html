Compliance News 11/2026

Consolidating Debt to an Interest Only Mortgage
11 June 2026

(please note that Compliance News 10/2026 was issued to Equity Release Advisers only)

Background

We've known for quite some time that the FCA are concerned with clients spending the equity in their property in instances such as purchasing fast depreciating assets like cars. They are also concerned where equity is used to clear unsecured debt – Debt Consolidation. These concerns, and our rules below, apply to both Residential and BTL mortgages.

Our Current Stance

We currently allow clients to consolidate debt to their mortgage, so long as the client fully understands the risks and implications of adding the unsecured debt to their long-term mortgage.

We are still expecting you to generally discourage debt consolidation unless it is absolutely necessary, or where the client has an over-riding preference for this outcome. Remember, you can always arrange the new borrowing over a shorter term or suggest overpayments to clear the debt sooner – and this should be referenced in your suitability report or Acre notes.

We will no longer support Debt Consolidation to an Interest Only mortgage

Our stance is that you cannot consolidate unsecured debt to an interest only mortgage. In the very occasional instance where you feel you really must do it, you must first speak with the compliance team for a concession – each referral will be assessed on its individual merits.

For your information, the whole network has had just 4 interest only debt consolidation cases in the past 12 months.

Lifetime Mortgages

There are no changes to our rules with lifetime mortgages. This communication does not apply to lifetime mortgages.

File Checks

We will continue to check 100% of all Debt Consolidation cases as part of our record keeping compliance controls.

Further information

If you are uncertain about any of this communication, or require training on our record keeping standards, please speak with your Sales Manager or our compliance team.
