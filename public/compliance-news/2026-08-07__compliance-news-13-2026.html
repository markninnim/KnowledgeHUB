PMS APEX Protection Panel (Protection Advisers only)
7 August 2026

Background

We are making some changes to the protection panel used with selected insurers. The primary reason for this change is to further strengthen our governance arrangements following the FCA's recent focus on the protection market and our ongoing Consumer Duty obligations. An additional benefit is an improved initial commission structure for advisers who take indemnity commission, at the expense of the renewal commission.

Who does this affect?

This change only affects advisers who take indemnity commission on Life and Critical Illness (CIC) products. If you take non-indemnity commission (“on the drip”), this change does not affect you. There is no change to client premiums or product pricing.

What is changing?

Legal & General, Zurich, Aviva, Vitality and Royal London are moving from the PMS Zenith panel to the PMS APEX panel. Each insurer will implement this change on its own timetable, so there is no single go-live date. Once a provider has gone live, all new business through that provider automatically uses the new commission structure. Note that under the APEX panel there is no renewal commission payable after 48 months.

Why are we making this change?

Moving to the PMS APEX panel provides enhanced governance through the Bankhall (PMS) Protection Review Service, including an annual review, helping us continue to meet our Consumer Duty responsibilities.

Do I need to do anything?

No. The transition happens automatically as each insurer completes its implementation – it remains business as usual. Any policies within their 48-month clawback period will continue to have renewal commission paid after 48 months have elapsed. If you have any questions, please speak to Dan or Pete.
