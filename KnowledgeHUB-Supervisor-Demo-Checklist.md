# KnowledgeHUB™ Demo Checklist — Supervisor Features

1. **My Team (Supervisor Zone)** — open adviser cards/list view, show CPD progress bars per adviser, 🎂 birthday indicator, drill into one adviser's full profile.

2. **CSV export & transfer** — export the team list to CSV, show reassigning/transferring an adviser to a different supervisor.

3. **Compliance Log** — open the six REPORTING form submissions (Complaint, Breach, Conflict of Interest, Gifts & Hospitality, Self Sale, Whistleblowing), open one, add a note, mark status, show the adviser-facing red-dot unread indicator this triggers.

4. **Consumer Duty** — open a broker's Questionnaire Feedback / response cards, show flags and the AI-generated compliance summary.

5. **Case Flags** — show the Flag Log for a broker and how a flag surfaces in their compliance summary.

6. **Annual Reporting** — review a submitted Fitness & Properness questionnaire, show the status filter (submitted / not yet submitted) and auto-archive.

7. **Introducers & Other Business Interests** — open the supervisor review pages for both, show a submission with signature.

8. **Holiday approvals** — approve/decline a pending holiday request, show the clash warning when two advisers on the same approver overlap.

9. **Whereabouts** — view the team Whereabouts grid for the week.

10. **News Bulletins** — post a new story via "Add Story", show it appear on the Home scroller.

11. **User management** (if admin) — open the users table, show product pills, add/edit a user, CSV import/export.

12. **Adviser Dashboard Meeting Notes** — add a meeting note for a broker, show it pulling into their quarterly compliance PDF export.

*Optional closer if there's time:* API usage summary (admin), Help widget (Alex) answering a team-level question like "who has the most reviews."
