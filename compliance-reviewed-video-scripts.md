# Mortgage Video Scripts — Compliance Review

Reviewed against FCA financial promotion rules (fair, clear, not misleading) and MCOB mortgage promotion requirements. All 15 scripts are **approved for use once the corrections below are applied**. None are rejected outright — the issues are additions/caveats, not content bans.

## Two things every script needs (add to all 15)

1. **Statutory mortgage risk warning.** Any promotion referencing mortgage products/deals must carry this, verbatim, on screen (caption) for the duration it's visible, and/or read out at the end:

   > **"Your home may be repossessed if you do not keep up repayments on your mortgage."**

   This is a legal requirement under MCOB, not a style choice — it can't be paraphrased or shortened.

2. **Generic-info disclaimer.** These scripts give general guidance to a public audience, not 1-2-1 regulated advice. Add a short line at the end of each video (spoken or on-screen), e.g.:

   > "This is general information, not personal advice — speak to one of our advisers about your own situation."

   Without this, a viewer could reasonably believe they've received individual advice tailored to them, which creates a Consumer Duty / suitability problem if they act on it directly.

Below, each script is marked **Approved** or **Approved with edits**, with the specific fix noted. Full corrected text is given only where wording changes (not just the two additions above).
